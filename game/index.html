EMBER DUEL

A duel of cinders and nerve. Outplay the Cinder Fox, card by card.

How to play

  • Ember is your mana. Each turn you gain +1 max ember and refill it.
  • Click a card (or press 1–7) to play it. Attack breaks block first, then health.
  • Draw 2 cards every turn. Empty deck? Your discard pile is shuffled back in.
  • Reduce the Cinder Fox to 0 health to win. Lose all your health and the duel is lost.
  • Space ends your turn · R restarts · M toggles sound
